# PLAN: Remove Quiescence Wait from Hot-Reload

**Objective:** Delete `wait_for_quiescence` function and `QuiescenceTimeout` error, removing Arc-based quiescence tracking from the hot-reload flow.

**Purpose:** The callback-based model replaces quiescence waiting. Host destroys instances in Preparing callback, eliminating the need for runtime to wait for Arc::strong_count to reach 1.

**Output:** Clean reload.rs module with callback-only notifications, NativeLoader without quiescence import.
